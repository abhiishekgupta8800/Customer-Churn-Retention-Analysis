# Customer Churn & Retention Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn and retention patterns in the telecom industry using Power BI.

The objective is to identify key factors contributing to customer churn, understand customer behavior, and provide actionable recommendations to improve customer retention.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall customer churn rate
- Identify customer segments with high churn risk
- Analyze churn by contract type
- Analyze churn by internet service
- Understand churn patterns by customer tenure
- Identify major reasons for customer churn
- Analyze monthly charges by churn status
- Estimate revenue at risk from churned customers

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- DAX
- Power Query
- Microsoft Excel
- Data Visualization

## 📈 Key KPIs

| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Customers | 7,043 |
| Churned Customers | 1,869 |
| Churn Rate | 26.54% |
| Revenue at Risk | 139.13K |

## 🔍 Key Insights

1. Month-to-month customers have the highest churn rate.
2. Customers with 0–12 months tenure show the highest churn risk.
3. Fiber optic customers have the highest churn rate among internet service categories.
4. 1,869 customers have churned, representing 26.54% of the customer base.
5. Major churn reasons include network reliability, dissatisfaction with products, high prices, and service dissatisfaction.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Improve first-year customer onboarding and engagement.
- Encourage month-to-month customers to move toward longer-term contracts.
- Investigate pricing and service issues affecting Fiber Optic customers.
- Develop targeted retention offers for high-risk customers.
- Use customer value metrics such as CLTV to prioritize retention efforts.
